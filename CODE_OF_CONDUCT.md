# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should be useful, technical, and safe to participate in. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect differing experience levels and communication styles;
- protect private information and accept a clear "no";
- de-escalate disagreements and let maintainers keep discussions on topic.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of credentials or private infrastructure, repeated disruption, and personal insults are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, security reports, and project-related public interactions. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method listed on the [DarranLiu GitHub profile](https://github.com/DarranLiu). For a report that also contains a vulnerability or sensitive infrastructure data, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting flow described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Enforcement decisions consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected participants.
